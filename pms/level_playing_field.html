Methodology

  • APMI classifies investment approaches into only four broad categories: Equity, Debt, Hybrid and Multi Asset.
  • Under SEBI's rules, every equity strategy must pick one of three broad benchmarks. That official benchmark choice is used here as a style proxy (for example Nifty 50 TRI versus BSE 500 TRI).
  • The peer group for each Kotak strategy is APMI's own grouping: the same category, the same service type (discretionary or non-discretionary) and the same benchmark.
  • Within that group, only strategies that report a 3-year TWRR and have AUM of at least ₹250 cr are kept, so the comparison is against established books rather than very new or very small ones.
  • A Kotak strategy that fails either filter is still shown, with a note, and is added to the comparison set so its rank can be read against the filtered peers.
  • Customised variants are kept as separate cards and are not merged with the parent strategy.
  • Returns are APMI TWRR, net of fees and expenses, as on 31-Oct-2026. Periods longer than one year are annualised, as APMI reports them. A figure of 0.00 in the APMI file is treated as not reported.
  • The APMI IA performance table does not include benchmark index returns, so excess return versus the benchmark is not shown.
  • APMI does not publish volatility, drawdown or Sharpe ratio; those risk measures are not estimated here.
  • Rank 1 is the highest return. Percentile is the share of the comparison set this IA beat (100 = best). The top-quartile cut-off is the 75th percentile of returns in the comparison set. Quartiles are shown only when at least 5 IAs report that period. 5-year ranks use only IAs in the set that report a 5-year return.
